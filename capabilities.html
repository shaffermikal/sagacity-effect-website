What We Bid On, Who We Team With

Sagacity Effect bids as a prime on federal contracts across the NAICS lanes below. We team with qualified subcontractors to deliver the work.

NAICS Lanes

These are the codes we're registered under in SAM.gov. We can bid on any federal contract tagged with one of these NAICS codes.

NAICS Lane Typical Contract Size
561720 Janitorial Services — Bid + sub to specialized janitorial firms $100K – $5M
561210 Facilities Support Services — Bid + sub to maintenance + operations subs $200K – $10M
541611 Admin / Program Management Consulting — Direct delivery + sub for specialized expertise $100K – $5M
561990 All Other Support Services — Catch-all — flexible scope $50K – $2M
541614 Logistics Consulting — Bid + sub to logistics specialists $100K – $3M
541512 Computer Systems Design — Bid + sub to IT firms $200K – $10M
561611 Background Check / Investigation — Direct delivery $50K – $500K
541930 Translation / Interpretation — Direct delivery $50K – $500K

  • Subcontractor network already in development
  • National reach, Chicago-based principal office

For Vendors

What we look for in subs
